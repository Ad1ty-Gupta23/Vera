# NovaNest Electronics Support Policy

## Shipping

- Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days.
- Express delivery takes 1-2 business days.
- Orders can be tracked using the link sent in the dispatch email.
- NovaNest currently delivers only within India.

## Returns

- Unused products can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Products must include their original packaging and accessories.
- Opened software and personalized products cannot be returned.

## Damaged products

- Damage must be reported within 7 days of delivery.
- Support may request photographs of the product and packaging.
- Refunds and replacements are approved after review.

## Warranty and support

- Electronics include a one-year manufacturer warranty unless stated otherwise.
- Support is available Monday-Saturday, 9:00 AM-7:00 PM IST.
