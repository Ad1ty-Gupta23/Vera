When will my order arrive?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. Express delivery takes 1–2 business days. NovaNest currently delivers only within India. Track your order using the link in your dispatch email.

Can I return a product?

Unused products can be returned within 30 days of delivery, with their original packaging and accessories. Opened software and personalized products cannot be returned.

What if my product arrives damaged?

Report damage within 7 days of delivery. Support may request photographs of the product and packaging. Refunds and replacements are approved after review.

Is my purchase covered by a warranty?

Electronics include a one-year manufacturer warranty unless stated otherwise.
